# Event context: otaku.dev'26

- Event: Hacktoberfest Hack Day, hosted by MLH, organized by Commit Culture (student-led Tamil tech community, first event)
- Place and date: Vels University, Chennai, 24 Oct 2026
- Format: individual projects
- Judges: about third-year college students

## Schedule
- 9:30 to 10:30 Opening ceremony
- 10:30 to 11:00 Morning refreshment
- 11:00 to 1:00 Hacking begins
- 1:00 to 1:30 Lunch
- 1:30 to 3:30 Hacking resumes
- 3:30 Project due
- 3:30 to 4:30 Judging and evening refreshments
- 4:30 to 5:30 Winners announcement and swag distribution

Effective build time: about 4 hours.

## Prize categories
1. Best Open-Source AI Project: original project where open-source or open-weight AI is an important part of how it works. Examples: an agent skill, build with an open-weight LLM or SLM, or a model harness. Prize: Hacktoberfest 2026 winner DEV badge and MLH swag bag. Track info: mlh.com/opensource-ai
2. Best Use of Gemma 4: lightweight open model, multimodal, accessed through the Gemini API. Ideas: multimodal assistant (text and images), focused AI tool, rapid prototyping. Demo: mlh.link/hackdays-gemma-demo
