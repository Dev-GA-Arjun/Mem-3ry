# Demo script (90 seconds)

Audience: third-year students. Keep it fun and let them take part.

1. (15s) Problem: "You save 500 reels and never watch them again." Show a phone full of saved reels.
2. (30s) Drop 2 or 3 screenshots on the phone page. Cards appear with category, place and tags. Ask 2 or 3 judges to screenshot a reel from their own phone and upload it live.
3. (30s) Ask: "cafes near Velachery?" Then a Tanglish query. Then tap "Plan my Saturday". Point at the tool feed: "this is Gemma 4 choosing and calling tools."
4. (15s) Close: "Runs on open-weight Gemma 4 with our own open-source MCP server. Repo is Mem-3ry. Stop saving, start doing."

## Pitch lines
- "You save reels because you love them. Mem<3ry makes sure you actually remember them."
- Pronounced "memory". Point out the heart.

## Safety net
- 5 to 6 prepared screenshots in samples/
- Screen recording of a successful run, in case Wi-Fi or API fails
- Phone hotspot ready
